# Klyp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026.

Klyp is a clipboard history manager for macOS maintained by Edi Hasaj.

## Clipboard history stays on your Mac

Klyp reads the system clipboard while it is running so you can find and reuse
previously copied content. Its local history can include text, rich text,
images, links, file references, the source application's identifier, and the
time an item was copied. This can include sensitive content you copy.

History, image caches, settings, and pinned items are stored locally. The Mac
App Store edition stores its data in its App Sandbox container. Klyp does not
send clipboard content or history to Edi Hasaj or any other service.

## No accounts, analytics, or tracking

Klyp has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, telemetry, or tracking. It
does not collect personal information remotely or sell or share clipboard
history. Links you choose to open, such as the support page, open in your
browser and are subject to the destination website's own privacy policy.

Apple manages Mac App Store downloads and updates under Apple's privacy
policy. Klyp does not receive your payment details.

## Permissions

Accessibility access, when enabled, lets Klyp paste the selected clipboard
item into another application and support global shortcuts. Permission does
not cause clipboard content to be transmitted.

The Launch at login setting controls whether macOS starts Klyp when you sign
in. File access in the store edition is subject to App Sandbox permissions.

## Control your history

You can delete individual history items and clear unpinned history in Klyp.
Pinned items remain until you delete or unpin them. Unpinned history is
limited by your configured history size. Quit Klyp to stop clipboard monitoring.
